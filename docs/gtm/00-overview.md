# Harvey — Go-to-Market Strategy

Written fresh 2026-07-21. This `docs/gtm/` set is a clean-slate GTM strategy built from
(a) the product as it actually exists in this repo and (b) a verified deep-research pass
on the market. Prior GTM docs (`docs/go-no-go.md`, `docs/outreach-template.md`,
`docs/free-tier-scope.md`, etc.) were **deliberately not used as input** — they are
reviewed only afterward as a completeness cross-check (see `10-old-docs-crosscheck.md`).

**Brand:** Harvey. **Domain:** `harvey-qa.com` (decided 2026-07-22, after `harvey.review`
was taken; consider the no-hyphen `harveyqa.com` as a defensive grab). See
`02-positioning-messaging.md`.

## Operator inputs that shaped this strategy

- **Ambition:** productized service now, scale toward self-serve product later. The
  free automated scan is the bridge — lead gen today, product wedge tomorrow.
- **Geography:** US-first, global-friendly. USD pricing, nothing that blocks
  international buyers.
- **Budget:** ~$0 to start (organic only). Paid options are documented as "when things
  work" upgrades, never as launch dependencies.
- **Timeline:** no hard deadline. Optimize for the strongest compounding engine over
  fastest first dollar.

**Brand: "QA leader in a box."** Harvey is the senior quality leader a small team can't
afford to hire, delivered as a repeatable audit — a whole-codebase readiness verdict
across ten equal dimensions, of which security is one. **The brand is codebase QA for
AI-generated apps** (operator direction, 2026-07-22), going deepest on the stacks AI
tools generate most — Supabase, Next.js, and Vite. We market the breadth, not security
alone (2026-07-21); security is an *acquisition hook* (it's what founders search for)
that leads into the QA-leader brand — never the product's frame.

## The strategy in five sentences

Harvey enters a category that already exists (audits for AI-built apps) but has no
equivalent to it — boutique auditors lack the tooling, scanners lack the depth, and none
positions as an outsourced QA function. The homepage casts wide on "AI-generated apps/code";
the **deepest coverage and the SEO wedge are stack-specific** (Supabase, Next.js, Vite),
where demand is verified and competition is thin. The wedge is a genuinely useful **free
automated scan** distributed through the Supabase/Next.js/indie-founder communities and
the SEO/AEO territory around AI-code-audit and security queries; it converts to a
**high-touch ten-module audit** — one readiness verdict across the whole codebase — that
no competitor matches. Distribution is 100% organic at launch: helpful community
participation, original research that earns press, content that owns specific search
queries, and Supabase-ecosystem partnerships. As the free-scan funnel proves itself,
reinvest the first revenue into automating the scan and selectively into paid channels.

## What "done" looks like for this strategy

Not a document that sits in a folder. A running engine: a live site with a working free
scan, a weekly content/community cadence, one flagship piece of original research in
progress, an application into the Supabase partner program, and three tracked funnel
metrics (scan conversion, scan→call, call→engagement). `09-launch-checklist.md` is the
path from here to there.
